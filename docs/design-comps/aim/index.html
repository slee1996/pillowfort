What the comps actually show
  • Classic AIM4.7/4.8 references show gray controls with white conversation/editing areas. Cream is a reinterpretation or later theme choice, not a universal AIM default.
  • Blue title bars and beveled controls are shared Windows conventions. AIM identity is more specifically running-man/buddy branding, grouped presence, away-state markers, inline screen names, buddy icons and personal expression.
  • The IM composition separates the transcript from a real editing pane with formatting between them. This is a stronger interaction comp than merely recoloring a single-line modern composer.
  • AIM4.7 preferences explicitly show an adjustable Buddy List Font set to Arial9. Do not assume all AIM typography was Tahoma or one universal typeface.
  • The customized XP conversation and the version3.0 sign-on come from a later retrospective. Their exact capture dates are unverified; do not silently combine them into a single default4.7 skin.
  • The 2010 iPhone examples retain buddy groups and identity while adopting mobile-native chrome. Preserve social cues rather than mechanically shrinking desktop controls.
  • For Pillowfort, adapt room-scoped people/presence and expressive conversation; do not inherit ads, stock tickers, persistent account graphs or outdated security.
