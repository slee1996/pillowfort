# Pilot invitations — founder sends individually

Drafts for a small, consent-based pilot, not an announcement or evidence of traction. Send only to an existing connection or someone who has welcomed this kind of request. Include the consent block with either invitation. Do not include a pilot key or room invitation in these messages.

## Friend-group host

**Subject: A fifteen-minute drawing break with your friends?**

Want to try a fifteen-minute drawing break with 2–5 friends you already hang out with? I'm testing Pillowfort.xyz: private rooms for friends to draw, talk, and play without setting up a permanent community.

You would pick a time, start at https://pillowfort.xyz, create a room, and privately invite your friends. Try “everyone draws the group's mascot in sixty seconds,” then do whatever feels fun. Keep the room invitation in your existing private group chat; it contains access credentials. Verify each friend's joining fingerprint through that trusted chat before approving them.

I'm interested in whether you can get going without my help, what gets in the way, and whether you choose to use it again in the next seven days. Help is available if you want it; needing help is useful feedback, not a failure on your part. I won't join or record your room. Please tell your friends this is a pilot; I only need your coarse setup feedback, not their names, messages, or drawings.

Would you like to host one? No pressure, and no need to send me a room link. The consent note below explains exactly what I'd record.

## Agent operator

**Subject: Let your agent host a room and invite you**

Would you try a small Pillowfort.xyz pilot with your MCP-capable assistant? The first task is for **your agent to create its own private room, invite you, verify your joining fingerprint, draw with you, and clean up**—not just join a room I prepared.

Start only from the public guide: https://about.pillowfort.xyz/agents. Choose local MCP if you want to run the participant's browser yourself, or the keyed hosted beta at https://mcp.pillowfort.xyz/mcp. For hosted access, I'll issue a new individual pilot key and send it separately through a private channel we agree on; never put it in a room link, screenshot, or feedback reply. OAuth clients still need that issued key for consent. Native WebMCP is experimental and isn't this cold-onboarding pilot's baseline.

Hosted MCP runs a real participant browser on Pillowfort/Cloudflare infrastructure, which can access that participant's decrypted content and in-memory keys. With local MCP, the browser runs under your control, but your model/client may still receive and retain tool results. Please use non-sensitive drawing content, review the guide's security section, and authorize only this room, you as the recipient, and the agreed private invitation/fingerprint channel. Pillowfort itself cannot send DMs; use an already-authorized messaging capability or privately retrieve the invitation yourself.

Allow a short setup session; local Chromium download time is recorded separately. Hosted connections have a ten-minute lifetime and two-minute idle expiry, so plan a brief drawing exchange once connected. I'd like to know whether public instructions are enough and whether you choose to use it again within seven days. No transcripts, screenshots, tool arguments, room links, or secrets needed. Interested? The consent note below covers the pilot record.

## Consent block — send with either invitation

Participation is optional. If you agree, I'll keep a participant code, cohort/connection type, consent date, coarse setup and assistance outcomes, timing buckets, friction category, and whether you report a separate use within seven days. I will not put names, contacts, room IDs/links, keys, fingerprints, messages, artwork, or raw tool arguments/results in the scorecard. Your contact details and the code-to-person mapping stay in a separate access-restricted founder contact list, never in this repository or telemetry. This is not consent to observe, record, publish a quote, or share your work.

I'll send one follow-up after the seven-day window. You may skip questions, stop, or reply “withdraw” at any time. On withdrawal I'll stop contact and delete your pilot row and pilot-only contact/consent/scheduling records within seven days, recomputing any unpublished outcome totals. I may retain non-identifying aggregate enrollment/withdrawal counts, but not your outcomes. Otherwise those individual records are deleted thirty days after your follow-up was due, even if you never reply; if you never start, deletion is thirty days after enrollment. Only anonymous cohort totals may remain. Existing personal correspondence and participant/model/provider-held content are outside this pilot record; I cannot erase other people's copies or promise provider deletion. Room cleanup is not universal erasure.

Published genuinely anonymous cohort totals may remain after withdrawal because they cannot identify or isolate your contribution.

If that works, reply: **“I agree to the pilot record and one seven-day follow-up.”** No reply means no enrollment. You can agree to participation without agreeing to any later referral or publicity request.

## Seven-day follow-up — send once, after the full window closes

Thanks for trying Pillowfort. For the seven days after your first attempt—not including the initial session—did you use it again for another drawing, conversation, or game with an intended collaborator? Please answer **yes within seven days / only after seven days / no / not sure**. If yes, was it in the first twenty-four hours, days two–three, or days four–seven, and did a reminder from me prompt it? No exact time or room details needed.

For the first attempt: did you complete the create → private invite → verified admission → shared drawing → cleanup flow **without live/custom help / with help / not complete / not sure**? What was the main obstacle: **finding the guide, install/runtime, client setup, authentication, capacity/expiry, invitation delivery, fingerprint/admission, drawing, cleanup, arranging friends, or other**? Just the category is enough—please don't paste private tool inputs, results, screenshots, room links, or content.

You can skip any answer or reply “withdraw.” No response will be recorded as unknown, not as “didn't return.”
